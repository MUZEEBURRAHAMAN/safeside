# Product Brief

## Goal
Help people make better, more confident food choices — without fear-mongering or food-shame — by turning a quick barcode scan into trustworthy understanding and a practical weekly plan.

## Problem
Shoppers want help judging packaged food, but existing apps lose them two ways: scores feel arbitrary or fear-based, and the tone makes them feel judged. A second cluster of pain is self-inflicted: dark-pattern billing and data gaps. Verdicts are also dead-ends — apps say what's bad but offer nowhere to go.

## Audience
Primary segments: the Skeptical Optimizer (~35%), the Protective Parent (~30%), the Goal-Driven incl. GLP-1 (~25%), the Curious Newcomer (~10%). Sizes are directional pending the survey.

## Value proposition
"Scan what you buy, understand the truth in plain language you can trust, and turn it into a plan and shopping list built around better choices — with none of the fear, shame, or billing tricks."

## Scope (v1 intent)
- **In:** barcode scan, transparent score + "why", pantry, manual weekly planner, swap suggestions, shopping list, honest paywall, AI fill/improve (later phase).
- **Out (for now):** AI photo-calorie tracking, cosmetics/personal-care, restaurant mode, social features, wearables.

## Success signals
- Users trust the score (state they believe it / find it useful even when they disagree).
- The scan→plan loop is understood and used (not treated as two apps).
- Honest pricing still converts; reviews praise the calm, transparent experience.

## Constraints
- Solo founder; AI-assisted build; commodity tech (distribution & trust are the moat).
- Licensing discipline (MIT/Apache/BSD in shipped code; OFF data is ODbL).
- Regulatory: avoid unsubstantiated health claims; "not medical advice"; allergen cross-contact disclaimers.
